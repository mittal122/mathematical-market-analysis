Quantitative Trading Strategies & Failure Modes

Volatility Arbitrage

Trading the spread between Implied Volatility (model input) and Realized Volatility (actual market movement).

Delta-Neutral Hedging

Maintaining Δ = 0 to isolate profits from Gamma (scalping) or Theta (selling time).

Beyond BSM

Advanced desks employ Stochastic Volatility models (Heston, SABR) for the volatility smile, and Monte Carlo simulation for path-dependent structures closed-form solutions can't reach.

Systemic Risk

These strategies are vulnerable to "Liquidity Black Holes" — during a crash, hedging gaps produce slippage that BSM's frictionless-market assumption cannot anticipate.
