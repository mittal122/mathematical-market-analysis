Settings

Everything here is stored only in this browser's localStorage — nothing is sent anywhere except directly from your browser to the API providers below when you use live features. There is no backend server for this site, so treat any key you paste here as visible to anyone with access to this browser (use a free-tier key with no billing attached, not a production secret).

Market Data — Twelve Data

Powers the Stock Insights page and the "Load Live Data" panel on the Calculator: live quotes, price history, and the realized-volatility calculation used as the σ input.

Get a free key at twelvedata.com/pricing (Basic/free plan is enough — 800 requests/day, real-time-delayed quotes). CORS is enabled on their free tier, so this page can call the API directly from your browser.

Pricing Defaults

Used to auto-fill the risk-free rate and dividend yield whenever you load live data for a stock — Twelve Data's free tier doesn't reliably expose Treasury yields or forward dividend yield, so these stay as editable assumptions rather than being silently guessed.

Monetization — Google AdSense

This site earns through Google AdSense Auto ads: once you paste your publisher ID below, every page loads Google's Auto ads script, and Google's algorithm places ad units on its own — no manual ad-slot wiring needed here. Sign up and get approved at google.com/adsense, then find your publisher ID (format ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX) under Account → Settings → Account information.

Auto ads only activates on a real, approved AdSense account visiting the live deployed site (not localhost) — Google's review process typically takes a few days after your first real traffic.
